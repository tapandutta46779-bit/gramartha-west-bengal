# Official Finance Rules

PMMY screening is sourced from the current Department of Financial Services page retrieved
2026-08-28. Categories are Shishu (up to Rs 50,000), Kishore (above Rs 50,000 through Rs 5 lakh),
Tarun (above Rs 5 lakh through Rs 10 lakh) and Tarun Plus (above Rs 10 lakh through Rs 20 lakh,
requiring a previously availed and successfully repaid Tarun loan). Dairy and other allied
activities are in stated purpose scope; collateral is not required under PMMY.

The official page does not set one universal lender interest rate, tenure or approval. Those remain
null and blocking. Output wording is always illustrative eligibility screening, never approved.

AHIDF is screened separately from PMMY. The Department of Expenditure temporarily continued the
Infrastructure Development Fund from 2026-04-01 through 2026-09-30, or until an earlier superseding
16th Finance Commission-cycle approval. The current AHIDF scope includes dairy processing/value
addition and the listed animal-husbandry infrastructure activities. Existing terms retained for the
temporary period include 3% interest subvention, lender finance of up to 90% of eligible project
cost, and a maximum eight-year repayment period including up to two years' principal moratorium.
These are scheme ceilings, not promised borrower terms. A live portal window, eligible project cost,
borrower margin, lender gross/net rate, security, underwriting and sanction must still be verified.
