# Data Gaps

HCES 2022-23/2023-24 and ASUSE 2023-24 unit data are acquired and transformed into sampled priors.
Blocking real recommendation inputs are now: current local dairy price history; sourced
productive-animal fractions and reachable-supply
calibration; incumbent capacity; transport cost per litre; current venture configurations/cost
ranges; and lender-specific rate/tenure/underwriting.

The survey priors do not by themselves identify locality demand or incumbent capacity. HCES needs a
documented small-area/population allocation step, while ASUSE remains district/sector/NIC evidence.
The raw unit records are restricted to the applicant and cannot be redistributed publicly.

Current LGD/PIN crosswalk, Mission Antyodaya/facility integrations, AGMARKNET/e-NAM processing,
and broader sector adapters are not complete. No missing item is substituted with synthetic data.
